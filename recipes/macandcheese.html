Mac and Cheese

Home

Description

This is the best homemade Mac and Cheese you will ever taste...

Ingredients

  • Macaroni noodles
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Salt and peppee

Steps

  1. Boil the macaroni noodles until tender,then drain
  2. Melt butter and stir in milk
  3. Add cheese and stir until melted and smooth
  4. Mix the cheese sauce into the macaroni and season with salt and pepper
